How to Add HR Portal to Your Phone

You don't need to type the URL every time! Save it as a phone app in 2 steps:

On iPhone (Safari):
  1. Open this link in Safari browser.
  2. Tap the Share button (box with upward arrow at bottom).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add. A Techbizz HR app icon appears on your home screen!
On Android (Chrome):
  1. Open this link in Google Chrome.
  2. Tap the 3 vertical dots (top right corner).
  3. Select "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app").
  4. Tap Install. Done!
