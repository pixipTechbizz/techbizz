Certifications

ISO 9001 Certified

TechBizz is proud to be ISO 9001 Certified, a testament to our unwavering commitment to quality, efficiency, and customer satisfaction. This certification signifies that our LED display solutions and CCTV security systems meet international standards of excellence in design, development, and service delivery.

Conformité Européenne (CE) certification

Techbizz is CE Certified, ensuring that our LED display solutions and CCTV security systems comply with European safety, health, and environmental standards. This certification demonstrates our commitment to delivering high-quality, safe, and eco-friendly digital solutions that meet global regulatory requirements.

Federal Communications Commission's (FCC) certification

TechBizz is FCC Certified, signifying our LED display solutions and CCTV security systems comply with stringent Federal Communications Commission (FCC) regulations for electromagnetic interference (EMI) and radio frequency (RF) emissions. This certification ensures that our products meet U.S. safety and operational standards, making them reliable and secure for diverse applications.

RoHS Restriction of Hazardous Substances Certificate

TechBizz is proud to be RoHS Certified, ensuring that our LED display solutions and CCTV security systems comply with the Restriction of Hazardous Substances (RoHS) directive. This certification guarantees that our products are manufactured without harmful substances, making them safe, environmentally friendly, and sustainable. Eco-Friendly Manufacturing – Free from hazardous materials like lead, mercury, cadmium, and other toxic substances.

Certificate of incorporation

TechBizz is officially incorporated, marking our legitimate presence as a trusted provider of LED display solutions and CCTV security systems. Our Certificate of Incorporation signifies our legal foundation, enabling us to operate as a recognized business entity committed to innovation, quality, and excellence in digital display and security solutions.
