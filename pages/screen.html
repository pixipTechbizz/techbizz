Types of Led screen

At TechBizz LED Solutions, we provide a variety of LED screens designed for different environments and applications. The choice of an LED screen depends on its installation type, structural design, and intended usage. Some are designed for permanent installation, while others are portable and modular. Additionally, advanced LED technologies allow for transparent and flexible displays to create immersive visual experiences.

Indoor Led Screens

Indoor LED screens are primarily used in shopping malls, control rooms, corporate offices, and conference halls. They are designed for environments where ambient lighting can be controlled, so they do not need to be extremely bright. Typically, indoor LED screens have lower brightness levels because they do not need to compete with sunlight. Instead, they focus on providing high resolution and sharp image quality for close viewing distances.

  • Lower brightness (800-1500 nits):Since indoor lighting is controlled, high brightness is unnecessary.
  • High resolution (P0.9 � P4 pixel pitch) :Ensures sharp images and detailed visuals for close viewing.
  • Thin & Lightweight Design: Allows for easy wall mounting or integration into conference setups.
  • Energy Efficient: Consumes less power compared to outdoor LED screens.

Outdoor Led Screens

Outdoor LED screens are designed for stadiums, digital billboards, roadside advertising, and outdoor event displays. Unlike indoor screens, outdoor LED displays need to withstand harsh weather conditions such as rain, dust, and extreme temperatures. To ensure durability, they are built with weatherproof enclosures and typically have an IP65 or higher rating, which protects them from water and dust.

  • High Brightness (5000-10,000 nits):Ensures visibility under direct sunlight.
  • Weatherproof (IP65 or higher) : Resistant to rain, dust, and extreme temperatures.
  • Large Pixel Pitch (P6 � P10): Designed for long-distance viewing.
  • Durability & Longevity: Built with robust materials for extended outdoor use.

Transparent LED Screens

Transparent LED screens offer a futuristic display solution that allows users to see through the screen while still displaying digital content. These screens are commonly used in retail stores, glass buildings, shopping malls, airports, and corporate offices, where they create eye-catching visuals without blocking natural light or visibility.

  • 50-70% Transparency:Allows natural light to pass through while displaying content..
  • Ultra-thin & Lightweight : Easy to install on glass walls and windows.
  • High Brightness & Visibility: Ensures clear content without obstructing the view.
  • Energy Efficient: Consumes less power than traditional LED screens.

Flexible LED Screens

Flexible LED screens take digital displays to the next level by allowing bending, curving, and shaping into unique forms. These screens are commonly used in creative installations, museums, exhibition halls, and stage backdrops, where traditional flat LED panels would not be suitable.

  • Bendable & Customizable: Can be shaped into cylindrical, curved, or wave-like designs..
  • Soft PCB Material : Allows flexibility without affecting screen performance.
  • Seamless Display: Ensures smooth transitions without visible gaps.
  • Lightweight & Thin: Makes installation easier in creative spaces.
