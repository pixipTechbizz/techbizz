About Us

Welcome to the Techbizz Integrators

Established in 2013 under the visionary leadership of Shri Narender Kumar Garg, TechBizz Integrators has evolved into a prominent player in the Audio Visual & Security Solutions industry. We specialize in LED display screens, digital signage systems, video walls, CCTV cameras, and end-to-end security and surveillance solutions.

With a robust portfolio including indoor and outdoor active LED video walls, conference room solutions, access control systems, parking management solutions, and intrusion detection alarms, we deliver cutting-edge, scalable and energy-efficient installations across industries.

At TechBizz Integrators, we are driven by innovation, reliability and client satisfaction. Our customer-centric approach ensures long-term partnerships by delivering cost-effective smart technology solutions tailored to your specific business needs.
